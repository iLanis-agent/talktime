TalkTime

How long will your script take to say out loud, or how many words fit your time slot?

About this

Time = words ÷ words per minute. 130 wpm is the commonly cited average for formal speech, 125 to 150 is normal conversation; a 10-minute speech is about 1,300 words, or 1,100 to 1,700 from slow to rapid. Matches the published tables at wordstotime (500 words is 4m 0s at 125 wpm, 3m 51s at 130). Add 10 to 15% for slide transitions. Read it aloud once with a stopwatch, because real pace varies with nerves and audience. About
